PRIVACY

Parjamie collects no personal data

Support Privacy policy

The short version

Parjamie has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or to anyone else. There are no servers.

What stays on your device

  • Your name, as you type it on the start screen, and the name of a second player on a shared device.
  • The scoreboard: the names of the players, who won, and when each game finished.
  • Your settings: hints, sounds and house rules.

Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Playing on two devices

When you host or join a game, Parjamie finds the other device on your local network and connects to it directly. During that game the two devices exchange only what the game needs: the players' names, the game itself, and the scoreboard, so both show the same results. None of it passes through the internet or any server.

That is why iPhone and iPad ask for Local Network permission. Parjamie uses it only to find and talk to the other player's device.

Children

Parjamie does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions? Open a request.
